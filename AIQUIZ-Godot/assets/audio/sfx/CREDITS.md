# Sound effect credits

Every file under `ui/`, `game/`, `world/` and `ambience/` was made from the
sources below. All of them are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain
dedication), so they may be used, edited and sold in commercial games without
attribution. Credit is kept here for provenance, and because several authors
asked for it as a courtesy.

The files were trimmed, layered, pitch-shifted, looped and loudness-normalised
before export (Ogg Vorbis, quality 5, 44.1 kHz). Cue names, levels and the
mapping to these files live in `scripts/autoload/sfx_catalog.gd`.
