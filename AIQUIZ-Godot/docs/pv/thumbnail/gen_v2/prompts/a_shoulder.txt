Concept A - shoulder charge.
P1 bursts through the blue door toward the camera with a full-body shoulder tackle: torso leaning forward about 35 degrees, leading shoulder and upper arm aimed at the camera, the other arm pulled back, front knee driving up, back leg fully extended pushing off. The hat is tilted back by the speed.
Debris: the door explodes into a cone of chunky blue slabs and cubes flying toward the camera - a few HUGE slabs very close to the lens (out of focus, motion blurred, partly cut off by the frame edges), medium cubes around the body, and many small chips and dust puffs. Pieces are rotated at varied angles. A ring of dust at the feet.
