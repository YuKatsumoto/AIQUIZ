# Vehicle model credits

The shipped AIQUIZ helicopter is based on **Helicopter** by **jeremy**, available at [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/m/eb7b31pjGtQ), licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The source model is stored at `source/helicopter_by_jeremy.glb`. The game-ready GLB separates the recolorable body and rotors and adds an animated belly hatch for the character-drop sequence.
